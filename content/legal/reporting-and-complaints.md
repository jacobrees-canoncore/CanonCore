**You do not need a CanonCore account to use this page, and you do not need to sign in.** You can report

## How to report something

`[ ]` *Reporting address, once created: `report@canoncore.com`*

Tell us:

- where the content is (a link, or enough detail for us to find it)
- what is wrong with it
- anything else you think we should know

You can send us any supporting information you have. There is nothing you need to pay, and no form you
need to fill in beyond telling us those three things.

## Reporting an intimate image of yourself

If an intimate image of you, or of someone you are acting for, is on CanonCore, tell us and include:

- that the content is an intimate image
- that you are the person in it, or that you are acting for them
- enough detail for us to find the content
- how we can contact you
- that what you are telling us is true, as far as you know

**We will take the content down as soon as we reasonably can, and no later than 48 hours after we get
your report.** We will also take down any other content we find that is the same, or substantially the
same.

We may decide not to take it down if we think the content is not an intimate image, or that you are not
the person in it and are not acting for them. If we decide that, we will tell you, and you can complain
about the decision. **Complaints from someone who has made an intimate image report are dealt with ahead
of others.**

## What we do when you report content

1. We treat your report as a reason to suspect the content may be illegal.
2. We look at the content and decide whether it breaks the law or breaks our
   [terms of service](/legal/terms-of-service).
3. If it does, we take it down quickly. In practice this means making it private so nobody else can see
   it. We may also suspend or remove the account that posted it.
4. We keep a record of what we decided and why.

If the content is child sexual abuse material, we also report it to the National Crime Agency, as the law
requires.

## Complaining about something we did

You can complain to us if:

- we took your content down and you think we were wrong
- we warned, suspended, banned or restricted you and you think we were wrong
- you think we are not doing enough to protect people from illegal content
- you think we are not making it easy enough to report content
- you think we have not respected free expression or privacy

Use the same address. Tell us what happened and why you think it was wrong.

**If we agree we got it wrong, we put things back as they were.** That means making your content public
again, and lifting any restriction on your account.

## How long we take

| What you sent us | What we aim to do |
| --- | --- |
| A report about an intimate image | Take the content down within **48 hours** |
| A report about other illegal content | Look at it within **2 working days** |
| A complaint about content we took down, or a restriction on your account | Decide within **5 working days** |
| Any other complaint | Reply within **10 working days** |

These are the times we aim for. We may not send you an acknowledgement when your report arrives, so please
do not take silence as meaning we have not received it.

## If you are not happy with our answer

CanonCore is run by one person, so there is nobody else here to escalate to. If you think we are not
meeting our duties under the Online Safety Act, you can tell Ofcom, which regulates services like this
one. If content is a crime in progress, or someone is in immediate danger, **call 999**.

## Accessibility

We build this page and our reporting route to work with a keyboard alone and with a screen reader, and we
test for it. Automated testing does not catch everything, so we do not claim the page is perfect for
everyone.

**Reporting does not depend on this page working for you.** The address above is an ordinary email address,
so you can reach us with whatever you already use. If anything here gets in your way, tell us and we will
find another way.
