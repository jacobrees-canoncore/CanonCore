CanonCore is a service for keeping a catalogue of stories, the versions of them you can watch or read,
and orderings you write yourself. It is run by one person in the United Kingdom.

These terms explain what you may and may not do here, how we protect people from illegal content, and how
to report a problem or complain. If you only want to report something, go straight to
[Reporting and complaints](/legal/reporting-and-complaints).

## Who can use CanonCore

You need to be at least 13 to have an account.

You are responsible for what you put into your catalogue, including titles, notes and the arguments you
write to justify where something sits in an ordering.

## What you must not post

**You must not post anything that is illegal under the law of any part of the United Kingdom.** This
includes, but is not limited to:

- terrorist content, and content that encourages or supports terrorism
- child sexual abuse material, including links to it, and content intended to groom a child
- content that stirs up hatred against people because of who they are
- content that harasses, stalks, threatens or abuses a person
- content that encourages or assists suicide or serious self-harm
- intimate images of a person shared without their consent
- content offering illegal drugs, weapons, or the proceeds of crime for sale
- content that assists illegal immigration or human trafficking
- fraudulent content

**You must not post content produced by, or on behalf of, an organisation proscribed under the Terrorism
Act 2000.** If we have reasonable grounds to think an account is operated by or for such an organisation,
we will remove the account.

**You must not post content that is harmful to children.** This applies to everyone using CanonCore, not
only to accounts held by children. It covers pornographic content, and content that encourages, promotes
or gives instructions for suicide, self-harm or eating disorders.

## How we protect people from illegal content

We keep the time that illegal content is present on CanonCore as short as we can. Because the risks are
different, we treat three groups separately.

### Terrorism content

CanonCore has no way to upload images, video or files, no messaging between users, and no search across
other people's catalogues, so there is no route here for spreading or promoting terrorist material at
scale. Everything shared publicly is visible to us as well as to everyone else, and the amount of it is
small enough for one person to look through. If we find terrorist content, or someone reports it, we
review it and take it down.

### Child sexual exploitation and abuse content

You cannot upload an image, a video or a file to CanonCore at all, so child sexual abuse imagery cannot be
stored here. Text can still contain a web address, so we treat links as a real risk and review reports
about them quickly. There is no private messaging and no way for one user to contact another, so CanonCore
cannot be used to groom a child.

If we find child sexual abuse content, we take it down, we remove the account, and we report it to the
National Crime Agency as the law requires.

### Other illegal content

For every other kind of illegal content, we rely on the same three things: the service has no upload, no
messaging and no marketplace, so most illegal content has no way to reach anyone here; everything shared
publicly can be seen and reviewed; and anyone can report content to us without needing an account.

### Taking content down

**If someone tells us about illegal content, or we become aware of it in any other way, we take it down
quickly.** In practice we make the content private so that nobody else can see it, we keep a record of
what we did and why, and we may suspend or remove the account that posted it.

## Intimate images shared without consent

If an intimate image of you, or of someone you are acting for, is on CanonCore, you can make an **intimate
image content report**. The
[Reporting and complaints](/legal/reporting-and-complaints) page tells you how, and what to include.

**When we receive such a report we will take the content down as soon as we reasonably can, and no later
than 48 hours.** We will also take down any other content we identify as the same, or substantially the
same.

We may not take it down if we decide the content is not an intimate image, or that the person reporting it
is not the subject of it and is not acting for the subject. If we decide that, we will tell you, and you
can complain about the decision.

## Automated tools

**CanonCore does not use any proactive technology to detect illegal content.** There is no automated
scanning, filtering or content classification of any kind. Everything we do is in response to a report, or
to something we notice ourselves.

If this ever changes, we will say here what the technology is, when it is used, and how it works.

## Reporting and complaints

Anyone can report content to us, whether or not they have a CanonCore account, and whether or not the
content is about them. You do not need to sign in.

You can complain to us about any of these:

- content you think is illegal
- a decision we made to take your content down
- a decision we made to warn, suspend, ban or restrict you
- a belief that we are not meeting our duties to protect users, to let people report content, or to
  respect free expression and privacy
- our use of automated tools, if we ever use any

Full details of how to do each of these, what happens next, and how long we aim to take, are on the
[Reporting and complaints](/legal/reporting-and-complaints) page. We apply these terms in the same way
every time.
